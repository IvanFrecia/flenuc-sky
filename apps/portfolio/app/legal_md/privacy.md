# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-08-01  
**Operator:** Ivan Frecia / SkyLabs Developments  
**Contact:** ifrecia@skylabs-developments.tech

This policy describes how this website (the “Site”) handles information.

## What we collect

- **Contact:** If you email us, we process the content of your message and email address to respond.
- **Campaign pledges:** If you pledge (demo or paid), we may store tier selection, amount, timestamp, optional display name, and a masked or hashed form of email for fulfillment and fraud prevention.
- **Technical logs:** Standard server/request logs (IP, user agent, path, time) may be retained for security and reliability.
- **Payments:** If Stripe is enabled, payment card data is processed by Stripe, not stored on our servers. See Stripe’s privacy policy.

## What we do not do

- We do not sell personal information.
- We do not use pledges to create an investment profile or brokerage account.
- Demo pledges do not process real payments.

## Cookies

The MVP Site is primarily static/session-light. Essential cookies or local storage may be used for security or UI preferences. We do not run third-party advertising trackers by default.

## Sharing

We may share data with:

- Infrastructure providers (e.g., cloud hosting) under their terms;
- Payment processors (Stripe) when checkout is enabled;
- Authorities if required by law.

## Retention

Logs and pledge records are retained as needed for campaign fulfillment, legal compliance, and dispute resolution, then deleted or anonymized when no longer needed.

## Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may request access, correction, or deletion of personal data we hold. Contact **ifrecia@skylabs-developments.tech**.

## Children

The Site is not directed to children under 16. Do not provide personal information if you are under 16.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change. Continued use of the Site after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy where permitted by law.
